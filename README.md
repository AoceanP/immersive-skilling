# Immersive Skilling

Purely visual effects that make skilling feel alive. Everything is drawn on your client only: nobody else sees it, and nothing changes how the game plays.

## What you'll see

| Skill | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Woodcutting** | Each log arcs onto a crisscross pile beside you. Wood chips fly off the tree as you chop. |
| **Fishing** | Each catch flies into a basket beside you, with droplets splashing up from the water. |
| **Mining** | Ore bounces out of the rock onto a pile beside you, with dust and sparks on each swing. |
| **Smelting** | Your ore flies into the furnace, embers rise, and the bar slides out red-hot, then cools. |
| **Smithing** | The item you make appears on the anvil red-hot, sparks fly, and it cools to its normal colour. |
| **Fletching** | The item you make rises in front of you and turns, while shavings drift down. |

Each item you get also pops up with how many you got and its name, such as "+15 Arrow shaft". Items that appear grow in with a little overshoot, spin and slow to a stop, then shrink away.

## Where everything goes

- **From where it really comes from:** logs come off the trunk of the tree you are chopping (on your side), ore off the face of the rock, catches out of the fishing spot itself, bars out of the furnace's mouth.
- **Ingredients go in first:** bars fly from you onto the anvil and the logs under your knife. Then the product appears.
- **Into your pack:** finished goods don't just vanish. The smithed item and the bar cool, then fly to you; fletched items spin to a stop, then fly to you.

Effects still play with a log basket, fish barrel or bag open: the plugin replays the last item that skill gave you. They also work on a Sailing boat, where props sit on the deck and sail with you. Motherlode pay-dirt, amethyst, Camdozaal shards, infernal eels, minnows and aerial fishing catches are all recognised.

Cooking is left to the Immersive Cooking plugin.

## Sidebar panel

- **What's showing now**, with how full the pile is, plus **Clear props** and **Pause**.
- **A card per skill**, with a switch for each effect and a **Preview** button that plays it at your feet.
- **General options:** item pops, how many particles (Subtle, Normal or Lots), and when piles clear (Walk away, Bank or Logout).
- **Report bug** and **Suggest** buttons.

Every option is also in RuneLite's normal settings for the plugin.

## Fair play

Immersive Skilling only adds visuals for things the game already told you: the XP you gained and the item that arrived in your inventory. It doesn't change your character, other players or NPCs, doesn't show anything you couldn't already see, and doesn't automate anything.
